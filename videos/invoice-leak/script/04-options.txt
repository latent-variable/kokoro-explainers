Three options, with the agent's time estimates. Option A: switch the two routes off. Minutes, the leak stops, but no customer can open or change an invoice. Option B: an ownership check in those routes. About an hour, customers lose nothing, but the hole stays open meanwhile, and future routes must remember it.
