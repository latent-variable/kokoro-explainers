Three options, with the agent's time estimates. Option A is a firewall rule that blocks fast readers: minutes, but a slow reader gets through. Option B is an ownership check in those two routes: about an hour, and it closes the hole, but every future route must remember it. Option C is row-level security, where the database itself hands each company only its own rows: days, and a mistake can lock customers out. Doing nothing leaves every invoice readable by other customers, and it has already happened.
