Three options, with the agent's time estimates. Option A: switch the two routes off. Minutes, the leak stops, but nobody can open or change an invoice. Option B: add the missing check, so each route returns an invoice only to its own company. About an hour, customers lose nothing, but the hole stays open meanwhile, and every future route must add the check. Option C: row-level security, so the database itself returns only your company's rows. Days, and a mistake can lock customers out. Doing nothing leaves invoices readable and changeable by other customers.
