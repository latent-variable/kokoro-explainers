# This video's own bar

- The bar is premium: it is ShipShow's live demo to a hackathon panel, so it must look finished and made for this decision, not stamped from a template.
- The viewer is an engineering lead who is not a security specialist. Every term (authentication, authorization, WAF, STRIDE, row-level security) is explained in plain words the first time it is said.
- The four layers a request crosses and the three options are drawn, not listed as text walls; each option shows which layer it lives in.
- The video ends on the three options side by side with what each gains and costs, doing nothing accounted for, and one recommendation with its reason.
- Timings (minutes, an hour, days) are the working agent's estimates and are said to be: no critic should accept them as measured.
- The log shows reads only. The video never claims an invoice was changed.
