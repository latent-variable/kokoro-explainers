# This video's own bar

- The bar is premium: a panel should be impressed in the first five seconds and want to see what this makes. Generic slides, empty frames and stock layouts fail it.
- It sells the problem before the product: managing many agents means deciding on work you have not read, and that is anxiety. The video must make that felt, then relieve it.
- It must land for people who do not write code. At least one example is not about code, and no line needs a developer to follow it.
- The look matches the LA AI Hackathon page (theme `buildday`): deep space navy, ember, ice and iris, uppercase Space Grotesk headlines, JetBrains Mono labels. It must not copy the Hop logo or present itself as Hop's.
- Karpathy's words are attributed to him by name and stay true to `sources.md`.
- The project is written ShipShow everywhere; its address is github.com/latent-variable/shipshow.
